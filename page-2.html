The crossroads

Two Ways Forward

A wooden sign stood where the road split. One arrow pointed toward a forest. The other pointed toward the river.

I turned the sign over. The back was blank. Of course it was. I had never decided what should happen next.

That was the strange part: I had made this world, but I did not know everything about it. I wondered whether creating something meant controlling every part of it, or letting it surprise me.

A light flickered between the trees. Down by the water, someone was humming.
