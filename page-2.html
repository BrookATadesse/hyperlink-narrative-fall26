The Unfinished Map

A short fictional story about curiosity, by Brook.

I was supposed to be finishing something else. Instead, I drew a river in the corner of my notebook.

Then I drew a bridge. Then a village. Then a road that disappeared past the edge of the paper. That is usually how it starts for me: one small idea becomes a place I want to explore.

I wrote a question beside the road: What is out there?

Before I could answer, the lines began to move. The river made a sound. The paper smelled like rain. When I touched the little bridge, my desk vanished.

I was standing inside my own unfinished map.
