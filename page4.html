The Boatmaker

An old boatmaker sat on the bank, folding little boats from scraps of paper. Each one carried a word: Maybe. Later. Tomorrow.

“Where do they go?” I asked.

“Wherever the current takes them.”

I picked up a scrap and wrote What if? When I placed it in the river, it unfolded into a boat large enough to carry me.

“You can keep wondering from here,” the boatmaker said, “or you can find out.”

Across the water, I could see the village I had drawn. Farther downstream, the river ran toward a bridge at the edge of the paper.

I climbed into the boat before I could talk myself out of it.
